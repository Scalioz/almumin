FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your Umrah.

You need a valid passport (minimum 6 months validity), 2 passport-size photographs, vaccination certificates (Meningitis and COVID as required), and your flight booking confirmation. Our team will guide you through every document required.

Our standard packages are for 14 days — typically 7 days in Makkah and 7 days in Madinah. Custom duration packages can also be arranged based on your requirements.

All prices are per person based on double/twin sharing room occupancy. Single occupancy and family room options are available at slightly different rates — please contact us for a personalised quote.

Umrah can be performed any time of the year. Ramadan is the most blessed time, as one Umrah during Ramadan is equivalent to Hajj in reward (Hadith). December–February is also popular due to cooler weather in Saudi Arabia.

We operate in small, personalised groups of 20–40 pilgrims per batch to ensure individual attention and a spiritually focused experience. Each group has a dedicated Tamil-speaking Mutawwif guide.

Absolutely. We have dedicated female staff accompanying every group. Wheelchair assistance, priority arrangements, and special care for elderly pilgrims are standard in all our packages.
